It begins with what is there.

Look closely at how it is used, what has led to it, what surrounds it, and what has simply remained. The closer the look, the more the first question can shift.

Look at what affects what. A small adjustment can reach further than expected. Sometimes something needs to move. Sometimes it is better left alone. The work continues until what remains makes sense as a whole.

Then it leaves.

It is used, handled, placed, remembered. It finds its place in a life that can never be fully known. The task is to look closely enough, question what needs questioning, and make each decision count. What follows belongs to the life it enters.
